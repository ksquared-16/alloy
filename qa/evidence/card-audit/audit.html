  1. R1Header grammar is invariant. Icon, uppercase micro-label, optional status pill, insight, supporting. Every card, no exceptions.
  2. R2Whitespace separates; rules do not. One hairline exists in the entire family.
  3. R3Two person-row idioms only — open (Household, Children) and enclosed (Assignments). There is no third.
  4. R4Colour is state, never decoration. The surface is white and grey until something is amber, orange or mint.
  5. R5Depth is not height. Detail goes to a card-anchored overlay or a centered Focus Card with scrim. Compact bodies are row-capped at SUMMARY_MAX = 3.
  6. R6Cards assemble, never own. build<X>CardEvidence(context) → UniversalCard; cross-card work is coordination.requestFocus.

Nothing wide needs inventing. FocusPanelCardSpan = 1 | 2 | "row" already exists and span: "row" is already used by the canonical derivation for attention and primary_next_action. A wide, shallow card asks for 1023 px that the grid already grants. Densities micro | compact | standard | expanded are likewise already defined; the live surface is entirely compact.

Why the rejected pass read as a separate library

It used real class names, but at the wrong altitude: household__group and __row belong to the expanded Household, not the compact summary, so the lab cards inherited grouped-and-ruled chrome the live summary never shows. It then rendered NO OWNER / HELD / UNRESOLVED boxes inside the specimens — the live family renders a bare em-dash — and it made Complete / Needs attention / Severe / Empty the unit of design, which this family treats as tone on one composition, not four compositions.

04 · One canonical composition per card

Business Process Journey timeline span:"row" · 1023 × ~150

A single horizontal band of stage columns laid left to right across the full row, one column per Business Process stage, each reusing the What’s Next fact grid vertically: a state glyph and stage name on the first line (complete = bend-pine check, current = filled bend-pine dot with the name in 700, future = hollow grey ring with the name in grey), the date beneath in supporting grey, and at most one outcome line beneath that. Progression is carried by a 1px connector between glyphs — bend-pine behind completed stages, #e5e9ef ahead of the current one — which is the same “colour is state” rule the pills already follow. The current stage takes the Children --focused treatment, so the eye lands on it without a banner. Skipped, revisited and reopened stages collapse into View journey →; the band never grows a second row and never lists Current Work.

Health & Safety status span:1 · 507 × ~300

Four uppercase in-body section heads borrowed verbatim from RECENT ACTIVITY — Allergies, Medical, Dietary, Requirements — each followed by open rows in the Household idiom, no enclosure and no rules. An allergy row is the name in 700 midnight with the severity as its own line in --risk red beneath; that inherited emphasis is the entire severe treatment, with no banner and no count. Requirements are label-left / value-right rows where Received is quiet grey and Missing is amber, which puts enrollment-blocking gaps on the card without turning it into a second Readiness. Emergency contacts appear as one Household count chip, because Household owns that truth and this card should hand off to it. Empty state is one grey line, No health information recorded, plus an Add affordance.

Staff collection span:1 · 507 × ~280

Deliberately the closest relative of Household and Children in the set: open person rows, no enclosure, no dividers, using the exact Children row skeleton — 44px round avatar, name 15px/700, a relationship pill in the Primary / Guardian slot carrying Lead Teacher, Assistant, Floater or Enrollment specialist, and beneath the name a two-column label-over-value pair (Room / Infant Room, Today / 11:00–2:00). Why a person is relevant is carried by that pill plus the room line, never by category headings, so the card never grows abstract sections. Scope narrows to the child’s current room, schedule date and site; anyone secondary is absorbed into a trailing count chip in the Household stats idiom.

Attendance timeline span:"row" · 1023 × ~200

It projects the existing ChildAttendanceReadModel and adds no model. The middle band is a horizontal day track across the full row: an expected-hours rule in #e5e9ef with the actual span drawn over it in bend-pine, and above it the merged checkInOutTimeline and roomMovementTimeline as evenly spaced ticks — 8:04 Checked in · Infant Room, 10:15 Moved · Playground, 12:05 Moved · Nap Room — each rendered as the What’s Next fact pair. Because room_transfer is a first-class event kind with from_room_location_id / to_room_location_id, classroom movement is real truth, not an illustration. Beneath the track, a compact strip of the last five DayAttendanceSummary days. Actions are the What’s Next outline button row — Correct, Record movement, Check out — which is correct rather than convenient, since corrections are entry_type: correction | reversal events and nothing here is an editable field.

Billing status span:"row" · 1023 × ~220

The full row divides into three equal columns whose only separator is the 10px grid gap — no vertical rules — each headed by an uppercase in-body section head. Current billing stacks What’s Next fact pairs (Tuition $1,850, Subsidy −$600, Family share $1,250) with the family share as the one bolded value. Past due holds a single amber fact pair and the Pay now outline button; when nothing is owed it holds one quiet grey line, Nothing past due, rather than disappearing or becoming an attention block. Recent activity reuses the What’s Next activity rows verbatim with a right-aligned signed amount, capped at four with View ledger → beneath — the same SUMMARY_MAX rule Timeline already follows. Payer context sits under Current billing as two open Household-idiom rows, keeping payer identity distinct from household primary contact. Only the amounts and the past-due state carry colour.
