arrangement exists anywhere on the disposable estate either.

## What this means

A **50/50 split cannot be expressed** on any existing disposable fixture: a two-way split needs two
responsible parties and none has two. The proof cannot be weakened to fit — the instruction is
explicit about that — so the fixture has to change rather than the test.

A **single-party standing arrangement is buildable** on Certopp (one party, and a non-zero Field
trip at $40). It would prove the inheritance chain and that an explicit override replaces the
standing answer, but it cannot demonstrate a *split*, which is the part an operator reads at a
glance and the part the Alvarez thread is about.
