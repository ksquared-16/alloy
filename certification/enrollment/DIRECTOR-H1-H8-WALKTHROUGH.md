# Enrollment — your walkthrough (H1–H8)

Use **staging** (`https://staging.workwithalloy.com`), signed in as yourself. Use a made-up family —
not a real one — and Stripe's test card `4242 4242 4242 4242` (any future date, any CVC). Nothing here
charges real money.

For each step: do it, compare with **You should see**, then write a note. Mark anything that surprises
you as **Blocker** (can't continue), **Wrong** (works but incorrect), or **Rough** (works, but confusing).

---

## H1 · Your document becomes a form

1. Go to **Processing → Import document** and choose **your own** enrollment paperwork (any PDF, Word
   or web page you use today).
2. When asked *What would you like to do with this document?*, choose **Create a native form**.
3. Read the form Alloy built from it.

**You should see:** your sections and questions in your order; people sections (parents, emergency
contacts, doctor) shown as lists you can add to; questions Alloy couldn't place marked for your decision —
never quietly given to the wrong person. A plain "Phone" with no owner is left for you to decide.

## H2 · Put the form to work

1. Fix anything you want in the form (labels, answer types, which questions to keep).
2. Save it into a folder, then publish it.
3. In **Business Process → Enrollment**, add it to the paperwork the family receives.

**You should see:** the form in your folder, published, and listed as a packet requirement.

Note: ____  Mark: ____

## H3 · Invite a family

1. Create a lead for a made-up family with two children. Start the family name with **ZZQA** so it is
   easy to find and tidy away later.
2. Schedule their tour. In **Tours**, open the family, choose **Record outcome → Tour Completed —
   Interested**, and **Confirm outcome**.
3. In **Decision**, open the family, choose **Record outcome**, then **Offer spot** for each child: pick a
   room and confirm.
4. In **Offers**, open the first child: **Record outcome → Offer accepted**. Open the second child:
   **Record outcome → Offer expired**.
5. In **Registration**, open the accepted child and choose **Start enrollment**. Review what will be sent,
   then send it.

**You should see:** the accepted child in Registration, the other in Waitlist, and a private link created
for the parent. The waitlisted child gets no paperwork and no fee. While a child is still being offered a
place, Alloy will not send them paperwork — it says to accept the offer first.

Note: ____  Mark: ____

## H4 · Fill it in as the family

Open the parent's link (a private window works best).

1. Answer the conversation. Try changing an answer you already gave.
2. Add the people it asks for: a parent, two emergency contacts.
3. Use **+ Add another child** to ask about another child, or to tell the school about a new one.
4. Sign and upload what it asks for, then submit.

**You should see:** questions that read naturally ("Do you have a street address?"); your changed
answer kept everywhere; a sibling's household answers offered, not asked again; "Your request has been
sent to the school for review." for a new child; "Paperwork submitted."

Note: ____  Mark: ____

## H5 · Pay

On the family page, pay the enrollment fee with the test card. Then open **Financials → Charges**.

**You should see:** the fee for this child only, then "paid". In Financials the fee is labelled with the
child's name, and each sibling's fee shows its own child. No fee for the waitlisted child.

Note: ____  Mark: ____

## H6 · Check what was received

As staff, open the family and the child's paperwork.

**You should see:** each submitted form, the signature, the uploaded document and the generated PDF,
all on the right child — and only on that child.

Note: ____  Mark: ____

## H7 · Review and record

Open the child's case in **Processing**.

1. For each parent and emergency contact: **Add** it, or **Reject** it with a reason.
2. If Alloy says the email or phone already belongs to someone, either **Use this person** or choose
   **It's a different person — correct the details**.
3. If Alloy says someone with this name is already in this family (for example a parent who gave a new
   phone number), either **Use this person** or choose **It's a different person — add them**.
4. For a new child the family told you about: correct the name or birthday if needed, then **Create
   child** (or **Reject**).
5. Approve the case. You can still add or reject people after approving.

**You should see:** each person added once, to this child, with the right role; nobody from another
family added unless you chose them; nothing added that you rejected; adding the same thing twice changes
nothing.

Note: ____  Mark: ____

## H8 · Finish enrollment

In **Registration**, open the child, choose **Record outcome → Enrollment complete**, and confirm.

**You should see:** the child moves to **Enrolled**; the sibling stays where they were; the family page
shows the child as enrolled.
