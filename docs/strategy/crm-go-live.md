# CRM go-live: replacing a center’s legacy system

**Scope:** What Alloy CRM must support for a real childcare director to move off a general center platform (for example Procare) for **enrollment and lead work**. This is a product and outcome definition, not a build spec.

---

## 1. Target user

- **Childcare director** — owns pipeline, relationships, and decisions on who to enroll and when.
- **Enrollment and admin staff** — capture inquiries, follow up, schedule tours, and keep records current so the director can act with full context.

---

## 2. Core workflows (day 1)

- **New inquiry intake** — every new family interest lands in one place, attributed and actionable.
- **Follow-up and messaging** — staff can continue conversations and see what was said, by whom, and when.
- **Tour scheduling** — book and confirm tours without losing thread or context with the family.
- **Pipeline tracking** — clear stages from first touch through decision, so nothing stalls unseen.
- **Conversion to enrollment** — a defined handoff when a family becomes an enrolled household (even if downstream billing lives elsewhere).

---

## 3. Must-have capabilities

- **Communication (inbound and outbound)** — one thread of truth per prospect or family for email/SMS (and similar channels as offered), so replies and outreach stay tied to the record.
- **Pipeline visibility** — list and board views (or equivalent) that match how directors think: who is new, who needs a nudge, who is touring, who is deciding.
- **Activity tracking** — calls, notes, messages, and meetings visible on the record so any staff member can pick up the relationship.
- **Basic reporting** — enough to answer “how many inquiries,” “where are we losing people,” and “what did we close this period” for operational and leadership check-ins.

---

## 4. What we are not solving yet

- **Billing** — tuition, invoices, and payment collection remain out of scope for this CRM go-live.
- **Scheduling and attendance** — classroom calendars, staff shifts, and daily attendance are not required to declare CRM success.
- **Full parent portal** — a complete self-service parent experience is not a gate; families may still use other tools for forms or payments until those are integrated later.

---

## 5. Success criteria

**A director can manage all inquiries end to end without leaving Alloy** — from first contact through tour and decision, including outbound follow-up and a clear path to “enrolled,” with history and reporting sufficient to run the front door of the center confidently.
