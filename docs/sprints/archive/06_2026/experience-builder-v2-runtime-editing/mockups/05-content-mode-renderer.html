Content Mode
Editing happens where the content lives. The popover follows your selection and dismisses when you click away.
Renderers are typed and platform-owned. You pick from the closed catalog; the slot re-renders instantly with the real result.
Options are semantic (show cents?) — never font size or color. Typography tiers are platform-owned.
