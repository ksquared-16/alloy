# Operational Learning Platform

**Status:** Canonical Platform Specification

The Operational Learning Platform governs how the Trust Platform improves over time.

Its purpose is not to train artificial intelligence.

Its purpose is to transform successful operational reasoning into reusable platform capability.

Operational Learning improves Alloy.

It never memorizes customers.

---

## Core Rule

The Trust Platform learns from decisions.

It never learns customers.

Learning is always generalized.

Never personalized.

---

## Why Operational Learning Exists

Every reasoning operation produces information.

Most AI systems discard that information or store customer conversations.

Alloy follows a different philosophy.

Every completed Decision Package asks one question:

> **Did we discover something that should improve the platform?**

If the answer is yes, the platform evolves.

---

## Mental Model

```text
Decision Package

↓

Outcome

↓

Observation

↓

Learning Candidate

↓

Review

↓

Promotion

↓

Knowledge Asset

↓

Future Decision Packages
```

Learning improves future reasoning.

Learning never modifies historical decisions.

---

## Platform Responsibilities

The Operational Learning Platform owns:

- pattern discovery
- confidence evolution
- strategy optimization
- deterministic graduation
- knowledge candidate generation
- recommendation quality analysis
- learning governance

It never owns:

- customer history
- operational truth
- reasoning
- execution
- provider training

---

## Learning Lifecycle

Every learning event follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Observation

↓

Candidate

↓

Evidence

↓

Review

↓

Promotion

↓

Knowledge

↓

Graduation

↓

Platform Capability
```

Learning remains observable.

Every promotion is auditable.

---

## Observations

Observations are facts about reasoning outcomes.

Examples:

- Recommendation Accepted
- Recommendation Rejected
- Recommendation Modified
- Validation Failed
- Human Override
- Policy Exception
- Reasoning Timeout
- Provider Failure

Observations contain no customer identity.

---

## Learning Candidates

Learning Candidates are generalized hypotheses.

Examples:

- This document layout appears consistent.
- This policy mapping repeats frequently.
- This communication wording performs better.
- This validation rule is always satisfied.

Candidates are not platform knowledge.

---

## Evidence

Learning requires evidence.

Promotion never occurs from a single observation.

Evidence includes:

- Frequency
- Consistency
- Validation
- Human acceptance
- Operational outcomes
- Trust Score

The platform learns only from demonstrated patterns.

---

## Promotion

Promotion converts Learning Candidates into platform knowledge.

Promotion always creates:

- New Knowledge Asset
- Updated Strategy
- Deterministic Rule
- Validation Policy
- Classification Rule

Promotion is versioned.

---

## Graduation

Graduation is the ultimate objective.

Graduation means:

Reasoning is no longer required.

Examples:

```text
Vision Extraction

↓

Stable Pattern

↓

Deterministic Parser

Classification

↓

Stable Mapping

↓

Deterministic Rule

Prompt

↓

Stable Behavior

↓

Platform Capability
```

Graduation reduces future dependence on probabilistic reasoning.

---

## Confidence Evolution

Confidence changes over time.

Confidence improves through:

- Operational success
- Human agreement
- Validation success
- Deterministic confirmation

Confidence never changes historical Decision Packages.

Future Decision Packages benefit.

---

## Learning Boundaries

Operational Learning may learn:

- Generalized mappings
- Document structures
- Operational procedures
- Knowledge relationships
- Classification improvements
- Reasoning strategy selection
- Validation improvements

Operational Learning must never learn:

- Customer names
- Customer documents
- Customer conversations
- Personal identity
- Sensitive operational history

Platform memory remains generalized.

---

## Runtime Guarantees

Operational Learning guarantees:

- No customer memorization
- Versioned promotion
- Deterministic graduation
- Generalized improvement
- Replay compatibility
- Provider independence

---

## Frozen Decisions

- Learning improves the platform.
- Learning never stores customers.
- Promotion creates Knowledge Assets.
- Graduation is preferred over permanent reasoning.
- Every learning event is auditable.
- Historical Decision Packages never change.

---

## Anti-Patterns

Never:

- Store conversations as platform memory.
- Learn customer identity.
- Automatically promote patterns.
- Replace deterministic rules with reasoning.
- Train providers using customer operational data.
- Treat successful reasoning as permanent architecture.

---

---

## Platform Metrics

Operational Learning should expose platform metrics.

Examples:

- Reasoning Reduction Rate
- Graduation Rate
- Knowledge Growth
- Trust Score Improvement
- Deterministic Resolution Rate
- Recommendation Acceptance Rate
- Learning Velocity
- Provider Independence

These metrics describe platform maturity.

Not model quality.

---

## Constitutional Principle

The purpose of Operational Learning is not to improve artificial intelligence.

The purpose of Operational Learning is to continuously reduce the amount of artificial intelligence required by the platform.

Every successful reasoning should eventually become deterministic whenever operationally appropriate.
