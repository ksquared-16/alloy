Commit is the seam. Everything before it is disposable and consequence-free; commit is the one-way door into truth. It generalizes the approve_enrollment handoff and runs on the BPR Preview → Commit engine — atomic, previewed, outbox-notified, never overwriting history.
Workspace ↔ Focus Panel symmetry. The workspace plans across subjects (the Room × Day board); the Focus Panel plans one subject. Same loop, one level down. The planning card is a new reusable card type — it follows the read-only billing_preview template, then becomes write-capable when the edit substrate lands.

Invariant: a Plan in proposed standing survives every cross-workspace excursion. Planning never dead-ends.
